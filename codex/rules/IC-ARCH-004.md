---
id: IC-ARCH-004
title: Avoid starters
category: architecture
severity: suggestion
source: https://kt.dev
---

Avoid opinionated project starters, generators, and scaffolds that pull in an
assortment of dependencies and conventions you did not choose. The first few weeks of
a codebase are the most important code you will write; use that time to make
deliberate choices about structure and dependencies rather than inheriting someone
else's defaults.

---
id: IC-ARCH-010
title: Practice simple design
category: architecture
severity: important
source: https://kt.dev
---

Prefer the simplest design that passes the tests, expresses intent, has no
duplication, and has the fewest elements. Simple does not mean easy — it means there
is nothing extra. New abstractions, indirection layers, and configuration knobs
should earn their place by solving a problem that exists today, not one that might.
